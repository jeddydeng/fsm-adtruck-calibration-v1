# Third-party components

This is the vehicle owner's source archive, not a relicensing of all components.
Existing licenses and copyright headers are preserved.

- RoboSense rslidar_sdk, nested rs_driver and rslidar_msg: LICENSE files in their folders.
- Senyun GMSL PCIe card0 supplier snapshot: drivers/black_vehicle/vendor/senyun_pcie_v4l2. Supplier source/binary/encrypted files and notices are preserved; PROVENANCE.json identifies the approved module and source hashes.
- ROS dependencies: installed from ROS/Ubuntu repositories under their respective licenses.
- Python dependencies: installed from PyPI; their individual licenses apply.
- Vehicle-specific packages marked Proprietary retain that status.

No new blanket open-source license is applied to proprietary vehicle or supplier code.
